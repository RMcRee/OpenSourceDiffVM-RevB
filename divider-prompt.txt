  caddock arrangement is like this. four dividers, say A,B,C,D, paired up, A&B all respective pins connected, C&D all pins connected (e.g pin A1-B1, A2-B2 ... C1-D1 ...C7-D7), lets call pair AB as having pins AB1 to AB7 and
  we have CD1 to CD7 as well. The input divider is AB1 as input, AB2-CD1, CD2 is output and to MUX and to AB7.
  CD7 is to ground. to form /10 divider we connect AB3 to 14112 and CD3 to 14113, closing the switch via teensy
  will create div 10 ratio. Similarly Ab4 to switch and other side of switch to CD4, final arm is AB5<S>CD5.
  pin6 unused for all.
