================================================================================
              CADDOCK 1776-C4815 SERIAL-PARALLEL ARRANGEMENT
================================================================================

The Caddock 1776-C4815 is a 7-terminal precision decade divider.

PHYSICAL ARRANGEMENT (4 dividers: A, B, C, D):
  - Pair AB: Dividers A and B wired in parallel (all pins tied together)
  - Pair CD: Dividers C and D wired in parallel (all pins tied together)
  - Series: AB feeds into CD

Pin connections within pairs:
  A1-B1, A2-B2, A3-B3, A4-B4, A5-B5, A6-B6, A7-B7  -->  AB1 to AB7
  C1-D1, C2-D2, C3-D3, C4-D4, C5-D5, C6-D6, C7-D7  -->  CD1 to CD7

SERIES TOPOLOGY:

     HV INPUT ──[FUSE]──┐
                        │
                       AB1 (Pair AB input)
                        │
                        R
                        │
                       AB2 ─────────────────────────────── CD1 (Pair CD input)
                        │                                   │
                        R                                   R
                        │                                   │
                       AB3 ─── MUX36D04 S1A ──┐            CD3 ─── MUX36D04 S1B
                        │                     │             │
                        R                  DA─┴─DB          R
                        │                (tied together)    │
                       AB4 ─── MUX36D04 S2A ──┘            CD4 ─── MUX36D04 S2B
                        │         (only one              │
                        R          pair active            R
                        │          at a time)             │
                       AB5 ─── MUX36D04 S3A               CD5 ─── MUX36D04 S3B
                        │                                   │
                        R                                   R
                        │                                   │
                       AB6     (unused)                    CD6 (unused)
                        │                                   │
                        R                                   R
                        │                                   │
                       AB7 ◄──────────────────────────────  CD2 ──► OPA848 ──► TPD4E1B06 ──► MUX S6
                                (feedback)                  │
                                                            R
                                                            │
                                                           CD7 ──► GND


DIVIDER RATIOS (MUX36D04 address selection):

  Address 0 (A1=0, A0=0): S1A-DA-DB-S1B bridges AB3 to CD3 → ÷10   (±50V)
  Address 1 (A1=0, A0=1): S2A-DA-DB-S2B bridges AB4 to CD4 → ÷100  (±500V)
  Address 2 (A1=1, A0=0): S3A-DA-DB-S3B bridges AB5 to CD5 → ÷1000 (±5000V)
  Address 3 (A1=1, A0=1): S4A/S4B spare (NC sources)       → off

Note: Exact ratios depend on Caddock internal resistor values.
      Calibration required for precision measurements.


================================================================================
                     MUX36D04 DUAL 4:1 MUX (U1)
================================================================================

Low-leakage dual 4:1 analog multiplexer (<1pA at 25°C).
Each half selects one of four sources to its drain output.
EN is active HIGH.

MUX36D04 Pinout (TSSOP-16):
                    ┌────────────────┐
        A0  1  ─────┤                ├───── 16  A1
        EN  2  ─────┤                ├───── 15  GND
       VSS  3  ─────┤                ├───── 14  VDD
       S1A  4  ─────┤   MUX36D04    ├───── 13  S1B
       S2A  5  ─────┤   (TSSOP-16)  ├───── 12  S2B
       S3A  6  ─────┤                ├───── 11  S3B
       S4A  7  ─────┤                ├───── 10  S4B
        DA  8  ─────┤                ├───── 9   DB
                    └────────────────┘

Signal Connections:
  MUX_A sources (AB divider taps):
    Pin 4  (S1A) ────────────► AB3 (÷10 tap)
    Pin 5  (S2A) ────────────► AB4 (÷100 tap)
    Pin 6  (S3A) ────────────► AB5 (÷1000 tap)
    Pin 7  (S4A) ────────────► NC (spare)

  MUX_B sources (CD divider taps):
    Pin 13 (S1B) ────────────► CD3 (÷10 tap)
    Pin 12 (S2B) ────────────► CD4 (÷100 tap)
    Pin 11 (S3B) ────────────► CD5 (÷1000 tap)
    Pin 10 (S4B) ────────────► NC (spare)

  Drains (tied together to bridge selected taps):
    Pin 8  (DA) ─────────────┬──────► Pin 9 (DB)
                             │
                        (bridge wire)

Control Connections:
  Pin 1  (A0) ──────────────► Teensy Pin 26 (DIVMUX_A0)
  Pin 16 (A1) ──────────────► Teensy Pin 27 (DIVMUX_A1)
  Pin 2  (EN) ──────────────► VDD (+15V, hardwired ON, active high)

Power Connections:
  Pin 14 (VDD) ─────────────► +15V
  Pin 3  (VSS) ─────────────► -15V
  Pin 15 (GND) ─────────────► Ground
  0.1 µF cap: VDD (pin 14) to GND (pin 15)
  0.1 µF cap: GND (pin 15) to VSS (pin 3)


================================================================================
                    OPA848 VOLTAGE FOLLOWER (U2)
================================================================================

Wideband unity-gain buffer isolates divider output impedance
from MUX36D08 and downstream signal chain.

OPA848 Pinout (SOIC-8):
                    ┌──────────────┐
        NC  1  ─────┤              ├───── 8  NC
       IN-  2  ─────┤              ├───── 7  +VS
       IN+  3  ─────┤   OPA848    ├───── 6  OUT
       -VS  4  ─────┤              ├───── 5  NC
                    └──────────────┘

Signal Connections (unity-gain follower):
  Pin 3  (IN+) ─────────────► CD2 (divider output node)
  Pin 2  (IN-) ─────────────► Pin 6 (OUT) (feedback for unity gain)
  Pin 6  (OUT) ─────────────► TPD4E1B06 Pin 1 (IO1)

Power Connections:
  Pin 7  (+VS) ─────────────► +15V
  Pin 4  (-VS) ─────────────► -15V
  0.1 µF cap: +VS (pin 7) to GND
  0.1 µF cap: GND to -VS (pin 4)
  10 µF tantalum: +VS to GND (bulk decoupling)

Unused: Pins 1, 5, 8 → NC


================================================================================
                    TPD4E1B06 ESD PROTECTION (U3)
================================================================================

4-channel ultra-low leakage ESD protection device.
Leakage: <50pA at 5V (critical for nanovolt measurements)

TPD4E1B06 Pinout (SOT-363):
                    ┌────────────────┐
         IO1   1 ───┤                ├─── 6   VCC
         IO2   2 ───┤   TPD4E1B06   ├─── 5   IO4
         GND   3 ───┤                ├─── 4   IO3
                    └────────────────┘

Connections:
  Pin 1 (IO1) ────────────► OPA848 output (pin 6)
                            AND continues to MUX36D08 S6 input
  Pin 3 (GND) ────────────► Ground
  Pin 6 (VCC) ────────────► +5V
  0.1 µF cap: VCC (pin 6) to GND (pin 3)

  Pins 2, 4, 5 → NC


================================================================================
                         FUSE (F1: 0ADAC0200-BE)
================================================================================

Eaton/Bussmann high-voltage DC fuse.
Rating: 200mA, 1000VDC

Fuse Placement:
  HV Input Jack ──────[F1]────────► AB1 (Caddock pair AB input)

Mount fuse in holder external to stripboard with adequate voltage clearance.


================================================================================
                    MUX36D08 (U4, existing in system)
================================================================================

8:1 low-leakage analog multiplexer. EN is active HIGH.

Connections for HV divider:
  S6 input ◄──────────────── TPD4E1B06 IO1 / OPA848 output

Control (to Teensy):
  A0 ────────────────────────► Teensy Pin 5
  A1 ────────────────────────► Teensy Pin 6
  A2 ────────────────────────► Teensy Pin 7
  EN ────────────────────────► VDD (hardwired ON, active high)

MUX Address for S6 (HVDivider): A2=1, A1=0, A0=1 (address = 5)
Corresponds to InputChannel::HVDivider in firmware.
